We, {{MERCHANT_NAME}}, submit this response to the above-referenced chargeback,

difference exists between the goods delivered and their description. No such
difference has been identified by the cardholder, either to us or to the
issuing bank. The chargeback form itself articulates no specific deficiency;
it merely recites the formulaic ground. A bare allegation of dissatisfaction,
unsupported by particulars, cannot satisfy the evidentiary burden that the
rules impose upon a claimant.

return and replacement window set out in our published policy before
initiating this dispute. That policy — acknowledged by the cardholder at
checkout — affords an adequate remedy for any genuine concern, and the failure
